Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

What we keep

  • Your email address and any other identifying information needed to sign you in.
  • Your playthroughs: badges, items, Safari Zone areas, the Pokémon you've caught and what's in your boxes, including any nicknames and notes.
  • Anything you send us through the contact form or by email.
  • Analytics about how the site is used, to help us improve it.

What we don't do

  • We never sell or share your data.

Where it lives

Your playthroughs are stored with Convex, sign-in is handled by Clerk, messages from the contact form are emailed to us through Resend, and the site is hosted on Cloudflare.

You're in charge

Download all your data or delete your account at any time from Playthroughs, under Account. Deleting your account removes all your data.

Questions? Contact us here.
