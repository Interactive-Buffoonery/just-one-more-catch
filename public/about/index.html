About

Just One More Catch is a Pokédex for retro games. The Journey tab shows you what's available to catch where you are in the story - and where to find it. Or, if you'd rather not know, you can use only the Dex and track the Pokémon you've caught.

This is a family project, led by Sarah . It's a fan project, and not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company. Our goal is to share a fun tool with other Pokémon fans.

Thanks

  • Encounter data, sprites and badges come from PokeAPI, a free and open Pokémon database built by volunteers.
  • Color themes are inspired by Rosé Pine, Dracula and Pokémon palettes from SchemeColor.
  • Icons are from Tabler Icons (MIT).
  • We also check our memory of Pokémon locations in games against various sources:
    • pret/pokered Red and Blue decompilation
    • pret/pokeyellow Yellow decompilation
    • pret/pokegold Gold and Silver decompilation
    • pret/pokecrystal Crystal decompilation
    • pret/pokefirered FireRed and LeafGreen decompilation
    • pret/pokeheartgold HeartGold and SoulSilver decompilation
    • PokeDaisy tables built from the decompilations
    • Bulbapedia walkthroughs, locations and items
    • StrategyWiki walkthroughs
    • Serebii forms, trades and events
    • Official strategy guides Nintendo Power, Prima and The Pokémon Company
AI Usage Disclosure

We use AI tooling to write code. Every change is reviewed and tested by a person before it ships. The Pokémon data comes from PokeAPI and various open source decompilation projects, not from AI. We also update the data as we play through games. If something looks wrong, please let us know.
