About

Just One More Catch is your Pokédex for retro games. It shows you what's available to catch at your point in the story, and where to find it.

This is a free fan project, and not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.

Thanks

  • Encounter data, sprites and badges come from PokeAPI, a free and open Pokémon database built by volunteers.
  • Color themes are inspired by Rosé Pine, Dracula and Pokémon palettes from SchemeColor.
  • Icons are from Tabler Icons (MIT) - thank you!
